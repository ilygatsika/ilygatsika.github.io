## Maths outreach

From time to time, I enjoy being involved in public workshops or school visits for making mathematical activities accessible to students and presenting the scientific profession to a broad school audience, with an interest in encouraging girls to pursue science in particular. Here is the material I produced for some occasions (that will certainly evolve):
- *The Archimedean Spiral: Uniform Rotation and Linear Motion* ([pdf in greek](/docs/koxlias.pdf)), 2017. Material for the workshop of Aristides Mavromatis and Apostolis Papanikolaou at Herakleidon Museum in Athens, intended as an activity for middle-school students and presented at middle-school professors. The aim was to propose a STEM activity for jointly introducing the physical modeling of the spiral movement and the mathematical notions used to describe the phenomenon (here periodic motion).
- *The Art Gallery Problem* ([pdf in french](/docs/art_gallery.pdf)), 2026. Material for a two-day workshop with high-school students
    during [RJMI](https://filles-et-maths.fr/rjmi/) (*Rendez-vous des Jeunes Mathématiciennes et Informaticiennes*) at Inria. This problem was the topic of my own presentation back at Euromath 2012, when I was a high-school
    student at Varvakeion high school in Athens. The present adaptation is a first attempt to introduce students to notions of algorithmic complexity used in scientific computing that are not covered in school curriculum.

I am also a volunteer at the french national program [Chiche](https://chiche.inria.fr) for introducing the profession of
    computational scientist to students and particularly to girls, beyond stereotypes.


## Open discussion

A place to share thoughts or resources I find interesting on current topics of
discussion.

- Questions and concerns on the scientific production and work cycles with the use of AI, as raised by the HPC research scientist Emmanuel Jeannot in [his essay](https://www.labri.fr/perso/ejeannot/ai-driven-science/ai-driven-science.html) in June 2026.
- On the impact of AI use in the scientific community and in the ways of sharing scientific work, as reflected in the announcement of [arXiv's policy update](https://blog.arxiv.org/2026/10/01/updated-rate-limit-policy/) on preprint submissions to the open-access archive on October 1st 2026. According to the available [stats](https://arxiv.org/stats/monthly_submissions), monthly submissions count for October 2026 was 52% higher than in October 2025.
- On the impact of AI in code development practices and the reasons why [Codeberg's terms of use changed](https://blog.codeberg.org/protecting-our-floss-commons-from-llms.html) to protect open source projects in the Git hosting platform. A documentary on the history and organisation of collective efforts to protect free software and intellectual property rights: [*La bataille du libre*, 2019](https://www.labatailledulibre.org/en/the-movie/) (in french).
