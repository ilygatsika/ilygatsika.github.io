## Maths outreach

I have been involved in public workshops or school visits for making mathematical activities accessible to students and presenting the scientific profession to a broad school audience, with an interest in encouraging girls to pursue science in particular. Here is the material I produced for some occasions (that will certainly evolve):
- *The Archimedean Spiral: Uniform Rotation and Linear Motion* ([pdf in greek](/docs/koxlias.pdf)), 2017. Material for the workshop of Apostolis Papanikolaou at Herakleidon Museum in Athens, destinated as an activity for middle-school students and presented at middle-school professors. The aim was to propose a STEM activity for jointly introducing the physical modeling of the spiral movement and the mathematical notions used to describe the phenomenon (here periodic motion).
- *The Art Gallery Problem* ([pdf in french](/docs/art_gallery.pdf)), 2026. Material for a two-day workshop with high-school students
    during [RJMI](https://filles-et-maths.fr/rjmi/) (*Rendez-vous des Jeunes Mathématiciennes et Informaticiennes*) at Inria. This topic is an adaptation of my own presentation at Euromath 2012, when I was a high-school
    student at Varvakeion high school in Athens. This adaptation is a first attempt to introduce students to notions of algorithmic complexity used in scientific computing that are not covered in school.

I am also a volunteer at the french national program [Chiche](https://chiche.inria.fr) for introducing the profession of
    computational scientist to students and particularly to girls, beyond stereotypes.
